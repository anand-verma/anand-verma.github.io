About Me

Yogi | Engineer | IIT Delhi Graduate

I grew up in the vibrant learning spaces of Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas—first in Kaimur, then in Pune. These schools shaped my curiosity, discipline, and teamwork. I topped the 10th class across all JNVs in India and represented my school in National Integration Meets and Yoga sports competitions. Those years gave me confidence and a strong sense of community.

I then went to IIT Delhi for Electrical Engineering. I represented the Fine Arts Club and explored creativity through art. I also pursued research in hardware implementation of neural networks, designing energy-efficient on-chip learning systems. Some of this work was published and presented internationally—my Google Scholar profile has the details.

After graduation, I worked at Qualcomm as an engineering intern and then joined Microsoft in the Cloud + AI Commerce division. There, I contributed to large-scale enterprise systems and learned how technology can impact millions of lives. These experiences shaped me as an engineer and problem-solver.

I am also drawn to the inner journey. I completed a Post Graduate Diploma in Yogic Studies at Nalanda, exploring philosophy, meditation, and balance. I enjoy painting, which lets me see the world in fresh colors. For me, technology, yoga, and art form a single tapestry—where innovation meets introspection.
